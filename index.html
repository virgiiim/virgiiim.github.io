01About

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Computer Science of the University of Pisa and a research associate at the KDD Lab of ISTI-CNR. I work in computational social science.

My PhD (National PhD in Artificial Intelligence, AI & Society track) looked at the duality of social media discourse: the same mechanisms of homophily and community aggregation that produce echo chambers can also build spaces of mutual help, like online mental health communities. I study both sides by combining attributed and temporal network analysis with text classification, semantic and affective analysis, machine learning and causal inference, mostly on Reddit and Twitter/X data.

These days I work on the co-evolution of people, social networks and AI: LLM-powered social media simulations (YSocial), the effects of recommender systems on who and what gets seen, and how reliable our conclusions are when platform data are incomplete or sampled. I also work on fairness and transparency in AI systems.

    02Research

    Five research lines tied together by online social platforms. Pick one to filter the publications.

    Online mental health communities

    How people disclose distress, cope and support each other in online mental health communities.

    Online polarisation & echo chambers

    How echo chambers and polarised debates form and evolve, and how sampled data can bias what we measure.

    Social media recommender systems

    How recommendation algorithms and network structure shape who and what gets seen.

    LLM agents & social simulation

    Simulated social platforms with LLM agents (YSocial) and agent-based models for controlled experiments.

    Responsible AI & transparency

    Bias, explainability and transparency of AI systems, including the detection and labelling of AI-generated content.

    The common thread: online social platforms

    All five lines study the same ground: Reddit, Twitter/X and simulated social platforms.
